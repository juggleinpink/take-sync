Edit (final selection)

While listening, switch to the take you want and press Enter at the edit point: "from here on, use this take." Tick "play the edit" to hear the assembled version, switching at every edit point. Move an edit point by dragging its line on the EDIT strip (hold Shift for fine moves) or with the ◀ ▶ buttons in the list (Shift-click: 0.02 s). With "play the edit" on, every move replays from 2 s before the edit point so you hear the new splice. Saved in this browser, per song.

How it works

The horizontal axis is the music, measured on the reference take (bold). Each bar shows what part of the song a take covers, so partials sit where they belong. Pressing a take's key (or clicking its row) switches to it at the same musical spot. Clicking a bar jumps there in that take.

space play/pause · 1–9, 0, q–p switch take · ←/→ ±3 s · ↑/↓ previous/next take that covers this spot · [ ] mark region · b back to region start · l loop · Enter use this take from here · f play the edit

Measure numbers ("~m.") are estimates interpolated from the starts_at_measure values in takes.csv; treat them as ±2–3 measures. They only appear once at least one take other than the reference has a measure number. Striped bars aligned poorly: check by ear. Takes are aligned by the notes being played; the alignment does not judge pitch.
